Training Programs

Four Disciplines, One Approach

Every program at Sun Training Center is built around the same idea: safe, progressive training that meets you where you are.

— Body —

Personal Training

At Sun Training Center, you train directly with Sunny Fong, the studio's owner. With over thirty-five years of experience designing exercise routines around each client's needs, Sunny brings the knowledge to help you get real results, safely and progressively. A trainer with that depth of experience simply has more tools to help you succeed.

— Energy —

Tai Chi

Translated as the "Grand Ultimate Fist," Tai Chi is a traditional Chinese martial art designed to cultivate one's chi, or internal energy. Strong chi supports mental and physical health alike. Through regular practice, students build flexibility, balance, and a lasting sense of calm. The Yang style of Tai Chi is taught in this class.

— Power —

Taekwondo

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art built around fast, powerful punching and kicking techniques. Its true value goes beyond speed and strength — it's about character, too. Master Fong teaches the five tenets of Taekwondo: courtesy, integrity, perseverance, self-control, and an indomitable spirit. Students learn taeguek forms, black belt poomsae, sparring, and self-defense.

— Mind —

Zhong Chi Chuan

Zhong Chi Chuan is an internal martial art created by Master Fong — his own holistic approach to developing chi and mental strength. It blends Tai Chi–style movement with slow and fast, soft and hard technique. For adults, the benefits include mental clarity, whole-body strengthening, and improved balance. Students learn forms, self-defense techniques, and conditioning exercises.

Our Story

About Sun Training Center

Three decades of experience. One goal: helping you move better, feel stronger, and stay consistent.

Master Sunny Fong

Master Sunny Fong has spent over thirty-five years in the fitness industry. He holds a bachelor's degree in Health & Fitness and a master's degree in Exercise Science, along with a sixth-degree black belt in Taekwondo certified by the World Taekwondo Federation.

During graduate school, Sunny studied Yang-style Tai Chi under Grand Master Lijun Cheng while conducting a research study on Qi Gong. He has been teaching the art for over thirty years.
